## Logo concept

Letter **B** built from a house silhouette, with a hidden checkmark in the
negative space.

- **Letter B** — BoaFie, bold commitment.
- **Checkmark** — verification, reliability, quality.
- **House** — home, comfort, the services that make life easier.
